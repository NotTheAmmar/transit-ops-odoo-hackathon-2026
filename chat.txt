The business rules are the heart of the application and should be planned before implementation.

Vehicle Rules:
- Registration number must be unique.
- Only available vehicles can be dispatched.
- Vehicles in maintenance cannot be assigned.
- Retired vehicles cannot be assigned.
- Cargo weight must never exceed vehicle capacity.

Driver Rules:
- Driver license must be valid.
- Suspended drivers cannot be assigned.
- Drivers already on a trip cannot be assigned to another trip.

Trip Rules:
- Only available vehicles can be selected.
- Only available drivers can be selected.
- Cargo weight must be within the vehicle's maximum capacity.
- Trip lifecycle:
  Draft → Dispatched → Completed
  Draft → Cancelled

Maintenance Rules:
- Creating an active maintenance record automatically changes the vehicle status to "In Shop".
- Closing maintenance restores the vehicle status to "Available" unless the vehicle is retired.

Fuel & Expense Rules:
- Every fuel log must belong to a vehicle or trip.
- Total operational cost should be calculated automatically using:
  Fuel Cost + Maintenance Cost + Other Expenses
